About this model · honest limits

1,000,000 dots are still superparticles, not a resolved Milky Way. Lifetimes are a mass–age clock, not stellar-evolution tracks. The lifecycle clock speed is a laboratory setting; speed 1 on 245 Myr only kills massive stars, and ~40 fits a ~10 Gyr solar life into ~250 Myr. Speed ≠ 1 is never calibrated. When the two-phase ISM is on, gas parcels feel a grid pressure force, ram drag, CIE-like cooling Λ(T, Z), and blastwave-delayed supernova heat. That is not SPH: there is no Riemann solver, no resolved Jeans mass, and no chemistry. Superparticle SN coupling is a free laboratory parameter because one slot is 10⁴–10⁶ suns. Star formation is allowed only in cold, dense, non-expanding gas. Energy change with the lifecycle or ISM on is not an error bound. The default galaxy is an exploratory warm disk in a live halo, not a calibrated equilibrium model, and a short run does not prove long-term stability. 2–5 galaxies share the same particle budget on one leapfrog tree. Barnes–Hut with two or five dense concentrations at the same θ is worse than an isolated galaxy. Birth-galaxy colors do not update when stars mix. The default 245 Myr span is a first passage, not a remnant, and not MW–M31. n_galaxies=1 is still the isolated revision-3 lab (same DF; generator stamped revision 5). N stays constant: gas ↔ star ↔ remnant. The worker stops at 120 h of wall time per resume window. Wall-time estimates scale from one measured 100,000-particle, 10-thread point; ISM and lifecycle factors are predictions; θ and softening are not in that model. Closing the laptop lid still sleeps the Mac.
