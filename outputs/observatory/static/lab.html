About this model · honest limits

200,000 dots are superparticles, not a resolved Milky Way. Gas is collisionless bookkeeping, not SPH. Lifetimes are a mass–age clock, not stellar-evolution tracks. The lifecycle clock speed is a laboratory setting; speed 1 on 245 Myr only kills massive stars, and ~40 fits a ~10 Gyr solar life into ~250 Myr. Speed ≠ 1 is never calibrated. The default galaxy is an exploratory warm disk in a live halo, not a calibrated equilibrium model, and a short run does not prove long-term stability. N stays constant: gas ↔ star ↔ remnant. The worker stops at 120 h of wall time per resume window. Wall-time estimates scale from one measured 100,000-particle, 10-thread point; θ and softening are not in that model.
